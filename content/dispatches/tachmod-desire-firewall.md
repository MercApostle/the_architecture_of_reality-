Nine of the ten commandments govern behaviour. The tenth — **Lo Tachmod**, "you shall not covet" — governs *desire itself*.

Why legislate a feeling? Because the ancient authors understood something modern neuroscience is only beginning to map: **behaviour is downstream of wanting.**

## The dopamine of comparison

Dopamine is less a pleasure chemical than a *pursuit* chemical. It spikes in anticipation, not satisfaction. Coveting — fixating on what belongs to another — keeps the system in perpetual pursuit with no possible arrival.

> Coveting is an energy leak disguised as ambition.

<!-- audit-cta -->

## Coveting in the enterprise

Organisations covet, too: a competitor's market, a peer's valuation, a trend that has nothing to do with their purpose. The result is strategic drift — energy spent chasing someone else's lane.

## Installing the firewall

- **Audit your inputs.** What are you consuming that manufactures envy?
- **Return to your own field.** What is genuinely yours to steward?
- **Practise sufficiency.** Name what is already enough, daily.

Tachmod is the last firewall because it protects all the others. Guard desire, and behaviour follows.
