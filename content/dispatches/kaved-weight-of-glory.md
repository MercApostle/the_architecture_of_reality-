In Hebrew, **Kaved** means *heavy*. Its noun form, *kavod*, means *glory* or *honour*. The same root sits beneath "honour your father and mother" — literally, *give weight to them*.

The ancient insight is profound: **glory is the capacity to carry weight.**

## Gravitas is structural

We speak of leaders with *gravitas* — a Latin cousin of the same idea. People feel safe around someone who can hold weight without collapsing or lashing out. That is not charisma. It is structural integrity.

## The epigenetics of load

Research on stress and gene expression suggests that *how* we carry load matters as much as how much we carry. Chronic, unrecovered stress expresses differently from challenge followed by genuine recovery. The vessel is shaped by rhythm.

## Becoming load-bearing

- **Increase capacity slowly.** Weight added faster than the structure can adapt causes fracture.
- **Distribute the load.** A covenantal team carries more than any individual.
- **Honour recovery.** Rest is not the opposite of strength; it is how strength is built.

Glory is not the absence of weight. It is the dignity of carrying it well.
